Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2025

Overview

Tools Hub is a free, open-source web application. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use the App and how it is handled. We believe in being direct: most tools collect nothing at all, and the only data stored is the minimum needed to support optional account features.

Guest Users (No Account)

If you use Tools Hub without signing in, no personal data is collected or stored by the App. All tool operations — unit conversion, QR generation, color picking, image resizing, and so on — run entirely in your browser and are never transmitted anywhere.

Your browser's localStorage may be used to save local UI state. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Account Users

Creating an account is entirely optional and only required to use the dashboard customization feature (reordering or hiding tools). If you sign in, the following data is collected:

  • Email address — used to identify your account
  • Display name — derived from your Google account or email prefix
  • Dashboard preferences — your tool order and list of hidden tools, stored in Firestore under your user ID

We do not collect, store, or have access to passwords (these are managed entirely by Firebase Authentication).

Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google)

Tools Hub uses Firebase, a platform by Google, for two purposes:

  • Firebase Authentication — handles sign-in via Google OAuth or email/password
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your dashboard preferences tied to your user ID

By creating an account, you are also subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's Terms of Service.

GitHub Pages

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may automatically log technical information including your IP address, browser type, and referring URL. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Cloudflare (cdnjs)

The App loads the QR code library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare may collect standard CDN request logs when this resource is fetched. No user content is sent to Cloudflare — QR code generation happens entirely in your browser.

How We Use Your Data

Data collected is used solely to authenticate your identity and save your dashboard preferences across sessions. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or analytics.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is stored in Firebase for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your data, open an issue at github.com/AndrewRayR/tools-hub/issues with the email address associated with your account. We will delete your Firestore data and Firebase Auth record upon request.

Children's Privacy

Tools Hub is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please open an issue in the GitHub repository: github.com/AndrewRayR/tools-hub/issues
